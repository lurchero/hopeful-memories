# PRD — Hopeful Memories Website

## Overview
Hopeful Memories, Inc. is a nonprofit at the intersection of storytelling, creative workforce development, and mental health. The website serves as the organization's primary digital presence — driving donations, attracting creative talent, and communicating impact.

## Primary Goal
Drive donation conversions (target: 3–5% conversion rate).

## Secondary Goals
- Attract photographers and videographers to apply for paid opportunities
- Engage corporate and foundation partners
- Communicate mission, programs, and measurable impact
- Build newsletter subscriber base

## Target Audiences
1. **Donors & Philanthropists** — Individuals and foundations who fund community impact work
2. **Families & Communities** — People seeking dignified, no-cost photography experiences
3. **Creatives** — Photographers, videographers, and storytellers seeking meaningful paid work
4. **Corporate Partners** — Organizations looking for measurable social impact partnerships
5. **Youth & Mentees** — Young people interested in creative education and mentorship

## Site Structure
- **Home** — Hero, Mission, Programs overview, Impact stats, Gallery, Get Involved, Donate CTA, Footer
- **Programs** (future page) — Detailed program descriptions
- **Impact** (future page) — Metrics, stories, reports
- **Get Involved** (future page) — Apply, volunteer, partner
- **Donate** (future page or external link) — Donation flow

*Phase 1 is a single-page marketing site (Home) with anchor navigation. Multi-page structure is planned for Phase 2.*

## Primary CTA
"Donate" — present throughout the page in hero, sticky nav, and dedicated section.

## Secondary CTAs
- "Get Involved" — apply as photographer, become a partner
- "Subscribe" — newsletter signup

## Content Sections (Home Page)
1. **Hero** — Full-width photography-driven, headline + dual CTAs
2. **Mission / Philosophy** — Why Hopeful Memories exists
3. **Programs** — 4–5 program pillars with descriptions
4. **Impact** — Key statistics and social proof
5. **Gallery** — Photography showcase from real programs
6. **Get Involved** — How to participate (donate, apply, partner)
7. **Donate** — Dedicated donation section with CTA
8. **Contact / Footer** — Contact form, newsletter signup, social links, legal

## Functional Requirements
- Contact form (Netlify Forms)
- Newsletter signup (Netlify Forms)
- SEO meta tags and Open Graph
- Analytics-ready (Google Analytics / Plausible placeholder)
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Fast load times (< 3s)
- Static export for Netlify hosting

## Constraints
- No popups
- No autoplay video with sound
- No heavy or distracting animations
- No sensationalized or exploitative visuals
- No overly corporate or templated nonprofit aesthetics
- Privacy-first approach
- Scalable structure for future programs and chapters

## Success Metrics
- Donation conversion rate: 3–5%
- Average session time: 2+ minutes
- Contact form submissions
- Newsletter signups

## Timeline
Phase 1 (single-page site): Within 1 month
Post-launch: Ongoing updates and iterations toward multi-page structure
